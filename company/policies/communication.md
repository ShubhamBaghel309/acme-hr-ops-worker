# Communication policy

- Welcome emails go to the new hire's personal email with the subject "Welcome to Acme". Include their start date, work location, manager's name and work email, and tell them to report to reception at 10:00 on their first day.
- Internal notifications go to work emails (@acme.example).
- Never include compensation, salary or bank details in any email.
- Write dates as DD/MM/YYYY.
- Sign emails as "Acme People Operations".
